# Grilling

Turn unresolved design choices into a bounded decision frontier.

1. Extract settled facts from the conversation and available evidence. Do not re-ask answered or discoverable questions.
2. Identify only decisions that materially change outcome, cost, risk, compatibility, or irreversible structure.
3. Ask one numbered round containing the currently independent decisions. Offer two or three mutually exclusive options, put the recommended option first, and explain the tradeoff in one sentence.
4. Wait for answers. Then either close with the settled decisions and remaining unknowns, or ask one dependent follow-up round.
5. Stop when the next action is safe and unambiguous. Return decisions to the orchestrator; do not build, dispatch, or write project files.

Prefer a stated assumption over a question when the choice is cheap and reversible. Never use interviewing as generic preflight or confirmation theater.
